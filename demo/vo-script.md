# VO script — "One kilowatt-hour's journey" (Deepgram aura-2-thalia-en)

## 01-hook (0–14s)
What if your neighborhood could trade sunlight? In Cedar Hollow, nine homes already do. Every roof a power plant. Every hour, an auction.

## 02-tension (14–35s)
Most rooftop solar is wasted — exported for pennies, while the evening peak is bought back at full price. The grid takes its cut both ways, and the carbon savings are never counted.

## 03-reveal (35–50s)
MicroTrade Pro turns the neighborhood into a market. Each home runs an AI agent that predicts its own supply and demand — then trades with its neighbors, automatically.

## 04-forecast (50–80s)
Every agent trains its own forecasting model on three weeks of meter history and live weather. Load and solar, forty-eight hours out, with honest uncertainty bands. Across nine homes, the average error is just over ten percent — sixty-two percent better than guessing.

## 05-market (80–110s)
Each morning, the agents bid into a uniform-price double auction. One clearing price per hour. Matched trades become forward contracts: the buyer escrows payment, the seller stakes a twelve percent bond. If the sun doesn't deliver, the bond pays.

## 06-ledger (110–140s)
Every contract settles on a verifiable ledger — signed transactions, Merkle roots, full history. Each delivered green kilowatt-hour mints a certificate, and retiring it makes the climate claim auditable forever. The same contracts live on Base Sepolia, a low-cost public chain.

## 07-payoff (140–160s)
Fifty-three kilowatt-hours, traded peer to peer. Nineteen point eight kilos of CO two avoided. Four dollars twenty-four, kept in the neighborhood — in a single simulated day.

## 08-close (160–180s)
Sunlight, traded next door. MicroTrade Pro — predictive peer-to-peer energy trading, built for IEEE ClimateChain. The code is open. The chain is verifiable. Come trade with us.
